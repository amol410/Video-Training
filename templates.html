Frequently Asked Questions

Are these motion graphics templates really free?

Yes. Every motion graphics template is free to edit in your browser, and the free plan exports 3 minutes of MP4 a month at 720p, with no watermark. Free export minutes are available in the US, Canada, the UK, Europe and other supported countries. Paid plans add longer exports, 1080p and higher, and credits for AI edits.

Do I need After Effects or design skills?

No. Templates open in the MotionVid editor in your browser, with nothing to install. Change the text, colors, logo and timing yourself, or describe the change you want and the AI makes it.

Can I use these templates for commercial projects?

Yes. Videos you make from our templates can go in ads, client work, YouTube videos and anything else, personal or commercial.
